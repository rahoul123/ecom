How do these edits reach my shop?
  1. Edit your products here. The work is kept in this browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back.
  2. Press Export products.js. Your browser downloads a file called products.js.
  3. Upload it to the js/ folder on your hosting, over the products.js already there.
  4. Reload the shop. Every page now shows your catalogue.

Photos are the one thing that does not travel inside that file unless you ask it to. Either upload your images to images/products/ and point each product at its filename, or tick Embed the photo and it will be carried inside products.js — convenient, but it makes the file bigger, so the panel tells you how big.
